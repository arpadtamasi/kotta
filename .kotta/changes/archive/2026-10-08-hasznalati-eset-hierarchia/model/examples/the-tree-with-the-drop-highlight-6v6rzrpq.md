# The tree with the drop highlight

## Given

A workspace with three actors, thirteen use cases, two overall rules and one rule with no place.

## When

The human opens the tree view on the board and marks *The teacher tries the course chat* as dropped.

## Then

The overall rules stand at the top, each actor's use cases below with their requirements, the unplaced rule apart; the requirements that fall out are marked as such and the ones that stay are marked as staying.
