# D-002 — Finding triage — channel-tagged intake, justified per-channel dispositions, channel-gated feedback, contract-gated autonomous handoff

## Decision

Resolves four design questions left open by D-001.

- **Channel is a tag, not a lane.** All intake lands in one queue; the source/channel (agent-review, human note, crash tool, MCP, user feedback) is a tag/facet you filter and group by — never a separate inbox. The tag also carries whether the channel is addressable (below).

- **Feedback is two-way but channel-gated.** While an item is still a finding (pre-disposition), a clarifying question can go back to the reporter — but only when the channel is addressable and the source was recorded (a crash signature is one-way; a user/MCP session is two-way). Post-disposition, an outcome status returns to the reporter. The finding stores its channel + addressability.

- **A disposition is the outcome of triage, and must carry its justification.** accept / reject / attach-existing / merge-duplicate / investigate are outcomes; the reasoning is the artifact (generalises accept-risk -> decision-record to every disposition). The legitimate disposition set may differ per channel.

- **Autonomous agent->agent task handoff is allowed, contract-gated.** If the spec is complete, who executes is fungible — the contract is the control. Gates: (1) only ready-validated tasks (complete acceptance + verification) may relay autonomously; underspecified stays human-in-the-loop; (2) verification proves the contract was met regardless of executor — autonomy is exactly as safe as verification is real; (3) high-risk / irreversible tasks still return to the human review gate. Every handoff is a logged claim-transfer event.

## Context

Continuation of the D-001 intake/derivation design conversation (2026-07-26). Four open questions — feedback direction, whether findings need a separate lane, who authorises agent->agent handoff, and which dispositions are first-class — were worked through and resolved as above.

The most important realisation came out of the disposition question: the operator's triage bottleneck is not the set of buttons but the lack of fast decision context ("nincs infóm gyorsan a döntéshez"). That is captured as its own capability finding (triage-assistant agent) rather than folded into a disposition list.

## Consequences

- New capability finding: **triage-assistant agent** (gather dup/related/code/blast-radius, propose a justified disposition). Depends on **F-006** (reliable derived reads) and benefits from dedup.
- **F-005** detail views must surface: the channel tag, the disposition justification, and the gathered related-context.
- Reinforces that **verification** is load-bearing — autonomous handoff safety rests on it; invest there before enabling autonomy.
- Still deferred (build-when-it-hurts): dedup/clustering machinery, the dedicated inbox ref (D-001), per-channel feedback transport.
- Still open: the `signal/ticket -> task -> goal` terminology (names, not the count of levels).
