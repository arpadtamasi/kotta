## Definition

Recorded new information from inside or outside the work - a possible bug, debt, risk, inconsistency - held with evidence until a human disposition.

## Usage

Anything noticed outside the current scope becomes an observation instead of a silent fix. It waits in "new" until validated and dispositioned.

## Non-examples

Scheduled work. A task. A to-do list item that is acted on without a disposition.
