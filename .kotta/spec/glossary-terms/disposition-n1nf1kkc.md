## Definition

The human-approved outcome of observation triage, carrying its justification: create-contract, attach-to-existing-contract, investigate, accept-risk, reject, or merge-duplicate.

## Usage

The reasoning is the artifact - a disposition without its justification is not a triage record.

## Non-examples

An automatic close. A severity label. A fix applied while triaging.
