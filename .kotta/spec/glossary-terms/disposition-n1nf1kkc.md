## Definition

The human-approved outcome of observation triage, carrying its justification: amend-spec, create-task, attach-to-existing-task, investigate, accept-risk, reject, or merge-duplicate.

## Usage

The reasoning is the artifact - a disposition without its justification is not a triage record. amend-spec is the primary constructive exit: the noticing changes the agreement, and tasks follow from the landed delta.

## Non-examples

An automatic close. A severity label. A fix applied while triaging.
