## Definition

A bounded, executable agreement about one outcome: an observable result, an explicit scope, acceptance conditions, and a verification method. The only entity that executes.

## Usage

Use for work that carries a product or deliverable promise a human will accept against evidence. A contract is signed before execution and closed only against accepted evidence.

## Non-examples

A "task" (work to do, with no acceptance or evidence). An issue-tracker ticket. An observation (information, not an agreement). A batch (a grouping, not an agreement).
