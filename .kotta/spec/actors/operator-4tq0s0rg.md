## Role

The human who owns product intent, priorities, and acceptance - typically a developer or technical lead directing one or more coding agents.

## Goals

Direct work larger than they can continuously observe without losing control of scope, execution state, provenance, or acceptance.

## Responsibilities

State intent. Decide every consequential gate: close, cancel, observation dispositions, decision records. Accept evidence, not reports. Answer open questions instead of letting agents invent intent.
