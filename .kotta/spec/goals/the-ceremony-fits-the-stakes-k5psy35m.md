## Outcome

Every gate asks for real judgement, and only that: the weight of ceremony scales with the weight of consequence. A gate that reads as "say yes again" is a defect, not a safeguard.

## Context

Measured before the model changed: a two-minute quickstart page and a schema migration traversed identical ceremony - five steps and two human gates - and every profile could only add weight. Uniform ceremony trains the approval reflex the gates exist to prevent.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: one gauge for all governed work; approvals unrecorded. Target: a spec-covered task crosses exactly one human gate, at close; new agreement is made only in the human line (observation, spec amendment, decision); every approval leaves a durable receipt.
