## Given

An active task covered by the accepted spec, whose execution reveals the export also needs an authentication header the spec never mentions.

## When

The executing agent raises an observation with the evidence and finishes what the coverage does cover.

## Then

The task never widens. The observation waits for the human line; on an approved amend-spec the agreement changes, lands on the base branch, and the landed delta yields the follow-up task.
