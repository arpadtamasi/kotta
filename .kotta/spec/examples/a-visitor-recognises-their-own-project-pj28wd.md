## Given

A developer maintaining a long-lived codebase opens the public Kotta page, having read the claim in the first viewport.

## When

They continue past the first viewport.

## Then

They find their own situation named among a small set of arrivals — new software, a rewrite of an existing system, and a codebase already under way — each stated in at most two sentences. The first viewport still carries the offer, the primary action and the control mechanism at desktop width, and the page keeps its responsive behaviour, keyboard access, JavaScript-free readability and freedom from serious or critical accessibility violations.
