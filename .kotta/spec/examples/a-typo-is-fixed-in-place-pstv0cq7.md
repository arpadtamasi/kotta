## Given

A defined task whose title misspells the feature it names, discovered before any claim exists.

## When

Define amends the title and the body; validation re-checks the sections and the coverage.

## Then

The task stays defined with its identifier unchanged, and history records an amendment - not a cancellation the record does not mean.
