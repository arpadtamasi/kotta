## Given

A defined task, "Caller chat task", and a decision that made it objectless.

## When

The agent proposes retiring it as obsolete, stating the reason but naming nothing that took the work's place.

## Then

The proposal is refused before any question is asked, naming the missing supersession, and the task stays defined. Naming the decision that replaced it makes the same proposal well formed, and only then is the operator asked.
