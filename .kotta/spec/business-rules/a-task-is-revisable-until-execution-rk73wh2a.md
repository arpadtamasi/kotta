## Rule

Until a claim exists, a task is a living document: define may amend a backlog or defined task - title included - and validation re-checks coverage on every amendment. Narrowing, correcting, or retitling never requires cancel and reopen.

## Rationale

A lifecycle that cannot absorb a typo or a narrowed scope routes honest corrections through a cancellation the history does not mean - and the record must mean what it says.

## Scope

backlog and defined. From active onward the brief is the executing agent's input: amendments go through the repair path recorded on the run, never through silent redefinition.
