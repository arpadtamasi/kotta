## Rule

Contract sign, close, cancel, and reopen, batch sign and close, observation resolution, and decision creation take effect only on an explicit human yes, given in the conversation, for that exact transition. Anything less - silence, a yes to a different question, an earlier unrelated yes, an agent's judgement that the human would agree - is a no.

## Rationale

Human intent and acceptance are the explicit authority the whole model rests on. Approval must be scoped and attributable, or delegation quietly becomes autonomy.

## Scope

Every approval-carrying mutation, on every surface - MCP elicitation and plain chat alike. Where no human is present, nothing is approved.
