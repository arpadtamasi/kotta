## Rule

Discovered information never becomes scheduled work by itself. The path is capture with evidence, validation, and a human-approved disposition - and even create-contract only feeds the define gate.

## Rationale

Silent scope expansion is how delegated work escapes control. Keeping intake decoupled from execution keeps the queue honest and the active work bounded.

## Scope

Every intake channel: agent discoveries mid-contract, human notes, external bug reports, connector feedback.
