## Rule

Product intent and accepted trade-offs come from the human. An agent that hits a real unresolved choice stops and asks; durable answers are recorded as decisions and referenced, not remembered.

## Rationale

The task corpus is the asset, and it is only trustworthy if every judgement in it was actually made by the party with authority to make it.

## Scope

Definition, execution, review, and triage. Open decisions block signing; mid-execution questions stop the run rather than being silently decided; recorded decisions travel into briefs.
