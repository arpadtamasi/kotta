## Story

As a developer or technical lead who has read the claim at the top of the public page, I want to see the situations Kotta is entered from — a new product, a legacy system being rewritten, a codebase already in flight — so I can recognise my own project in one of them instead of inferring whether the product applies to me.

## Value

A sharp claim earns attention; recognition converts it. Naming the three arrivals lets a visitor place themselves without reading the workflow documentation, and it distinguishes Kotta from tools that assume greenfield work.

## Notes

The public page is short by design: the situations are named, not explained, and the longer treatment lives in the repository README. This story adds to the page below the first viewport and must not displace what the first viewport already carries.
